How it works

FFmpeg runs compiled to WebAssembly directly in your browser. Your video is never uploaded to any server — all processing is local.

A two-pass method is used: first a colour palette is generated from your clip, then the GIF is rendered using that palette for maximum quality.

Settings guide

  • Width — output pixel width, height scales automatically
  • FPS — 12–15 is usually indistinguishable from 24
  • Dither Bayer — crispest edges, best compression
  • Palette Diff — best for clips with motion
  • Palette Full — best for static or slow clips

Smaller GIFs

  • Keep clips under 5 seconds
  • 480px width or less
  • 10–15 FPS is usually enough
  • Bayer dither compresses better than Floyd-Steinberg

Hosting
